# Decision 002: Initial Price $149

## Decision

Set the first public hardware price at $149.

## Options Considered

- $99: lower friction, weaker premium signal.
- $129: balanced entry price.
- $149: stronger premium signal and more room for discounts or bundles.

## Evidence

- Product visuals support a premium black-and-gold faith wearable.
- The product includes hardware plus a companion software experience.
- $149 keeps the product below mainstream premium wearable pricing while staying meaningfully above app-only subscriptions.

## Final Choice

Use:

```text
$149
Ships in 14-21 days
Companion plan expected after activation
```

## Reversal Criteria

Revisit if high-intent Search traffic reaches checkout but fails to convert because of price or shipping trust concerns.
